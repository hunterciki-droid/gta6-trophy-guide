    Roadmap to the platinum

    About 200 hours, difficulty 4/10, one playthrough. No online trophies, no mission replay, and free roam continues after the story. Cheats disable trophies and saving — keep them off.

    Stage 1 — Play the story 60–80 hrs · 20 trophies

    Finish Chapters 1–5 and the side-character strands as they appear. Do the one missable thing in the game here: the Chapter 1 meditation (see the red box below). Scan vehicles with WAiNK and study animals with binoculars as you travel — 200 vehicles and 100 animals are needed later.

    Stage 2 — 100% Completion Checklist ~100 hrs · 16 trophies

    After the credits the checklist opens at Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. It wants 100 optional missions, 200 vehicles scanned, 100 animals studied, 70 of 80 challenges, 300 of 397 stunts, all 8 bank vaults, all 39 gang operations, all 15 Target Decks, 15 of 18 meditations, 15 of 18 card sets, 30 of 40 races won, 10 of 13 race challenge golds, all 42 drone races, the underwater discoveries and both unique playlists.

    Stage 3 — Cleanup ~10 hrs · 19 trophies

    Whatever is left: the robbery trophies, the partner trophies, the one-off stunts and the money counters. Every quest giver tied to a trophy comes back after the story, so nothing here can be missed.

    ⚠ Missable trophies: 1 (potentially)

    Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot across Leonida. During Chapter 1, the morning after the mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen at Jason’s house, follow her to the balcony and choose Accept. It raises your meditation counter by 1 but is not marked as a spot like the rest, so it is still unconfirmed whether the trophy needs it. Do it anyway, and make a manual save first. Everything else can be earned after the story.
